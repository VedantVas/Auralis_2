About Auralis

Auralis – Smart Information Hub is a final year project developed by Vedant Vashishtha, Raj Vishwakarma, and Abhay Rajak with the vision of making learning smarter, faster, and a lot less frustrating.

The Inspiration

The idea behind Auralis came from Vedant Vashishtha, who was tired of switching between multiple tabs just to study properly.

Like most students, what started as "just searching one thing" often ended with 15 tabs open and complete confusion. That small everyday struggle inspired the creation of this unified platform.

Core Objectives

To bring useful tools together into one smart platform where users can:

  • Ask AI-Powered Questions

    Get simplified answers instantly without leaving the page.

  • Search Word Meanings

    Explore detailed definitions and phonetics seamlessly.
